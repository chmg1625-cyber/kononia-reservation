📋 Koinonia Rules & Regulations

We kindly ask all residents of iHouse and Carmichael RC students to read all the rules & regulations below 😊

📌 Reservation Instructions

  1. Reservations can be made from one week to one day before the date of use.
    Ex. If you want to use on 4/10, you can reserve between 4/3–4/9. Booking on the day itself is not allowed.
  2. Reservation is first come, first serve.
  3. When making reservations, please follow this format:
    Name / Room Number / Kakao ID / No. of People / Reason of Usage
  4. Each group is allowed to use Koinonia for max 2 hours per day, max 6 hours per week.
  5. Individuals are not allowed to use Koinonia — it is a community space (at least 4 people required).

📐 Rules When Using Koinonia

  1. Only iHouse residents or Carmichael RC students are allowed to use Koinonia.
  2. Do not lock the door and do not borrow or take anything from inside without permission.
  3. Please leave the door open for the next group to enter 🙂

📽️ Projector Instructions

  1. Plug the projector into the power socket to turn it on.
  2. Press the Start-Up button — the screen will come down.
  3. Connect the HDMI cable to the HDMI port on your device.
  4. Connect the audio cable to the audio port on your device.
  5. After finishing, press the Shut-Down button and unplug the projector.
If it does not work, please ask in the '25-1 Koinonia Reservation' open chat room.
😊 Koinonia is open everyday 8am–11pm. If there are NO reservations at a specific time, anyone is welcome to use it without a reservation!
